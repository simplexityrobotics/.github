## Welcome to Simplexity Robotics Github Repository


> Simple Is Scalable


Simplexity Robotics develops embodied AI products for practical applications, combining foundation models, iterative data collection and learning, and dependable robot hardware.

### Philosophy

**Simple Is Scalable**

The company emphasizes simplicity and scalability across research, engineering, and deployment. Its internally developed hardware and software combine world models with vision-language-action models to coordinate learning, data, and physical robots.

## Our Team

The team brings together specialists in AI, robotics, autonomous driving, software, hardware, and product management.

- **R&D locations:** Beijing, Shanghai, and Suzhou.
- **Full-time employees:** 116.
- **Employees holding master’s or doctoral degrees:** 65, representing over 56%.

Core expertise includes robot hardware, algorithms, and motion control, covering research, product development, and deployment.
